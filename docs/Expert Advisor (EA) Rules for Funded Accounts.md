
# Expert Advisor (EA) Rules for Funded Accounts

This report outlines the rules and policies regarding the use of Expert Advisors (EAs) on funded accounts for the following proprietary trading firms: FTMO, Fidelcrest, FXIFY, and Funded Trading Plus.

## FTMO

**General Stance on EAs:**
FTMO generally allows the use of Expert Advisors (EAs) on both their challenge and funded accounts. They state that as long as your trading is legitimate and conforms to real market conditions, there's no limitation to your trading style or strategy.

**Allowed EA Types:**
FTMO is "cool with using certain types of EAs" as long as their rules are followed. This includes:
*   **Standard Trading EAs:** Those using regular trading strategies like trend following or technical analysis, provided they stick to FTMO's risk rules.
*   **Swing Trading EAs:** EAs that hold positions for days or weeks to capture larger moves, as long as they don't interfere with risk limits.
*   **Algorithmic Trading EAs:** If the EA runs on a solid algorithm based on technical indicators or patterns, it's generally allowed, avoiding "shady stuff" like HFT or arbitrage.
*   **Scalping EAs:** Short-term scalping EAs are acceptable, provided they don't fall into the high-frequency trading category and respect risk rules.
*   **Risk Management EAs:** EAs designed to manage stop-loss and take-profit levels or adjust position sizes are allowed as they help maintain compliance with FTMO's guidelines.
*   **Custom Strategy EAs:** Custom EAs are fine as long as they don't involve banned methods and adhere to risk management rules.

**Prohibited EA Types/Strategies:**
FTMO explicitly prohibits EAs that engage in:
*   **High-Frequency Trading (HFT)**.
*   **Arbitrage** (including latency arbitrage).
*   **Martingale strategies**.
*   **Grid trading**.

**Third-Party EA Risk:**
While EAs are allowed, FTMO cautions that if you use a third-party EA, you run the risk of being denied a funded account if you exceed their maximum capital allocation rule. This is because if too many traders use the same third-party EA, it could lead to the firm exceeding its maximum capital allocation for that specific strategy.

**Key Rules for Funded Accounts (General):**
*   **Profit Target:** Typically 10% for the Challenge and 5% for the Verification.
*   **Maximum Daily Loss:** Typically 5% of the initial account balance.
*   **Maximum Loss:** Typically 10% of the initial account balance.
*   **Trading Days:** A minimum number of trading days is required during the evaluation period to demonstrate consistent performance.

## Fidelcrest

**General Stance on EAs:**
Fidelcrest permits auto trading systems and allows traders to use MT4 and MT5. They acknowledge a "Third Party EA Risk" in their rules, indicating that while EAs are allowed, there are considerations regarding their use.

**Rules and Considerations for EAs:**
*   **Third-Party EA Risk:** Fidelcrest lists "Third Party EA Risk" as one of its rules, suggesting that while EAs are allowed, there might be policies to mitigate risks associated with widely used third-party EAs.
*   **No Martingale Allowed:** Fidelcrest explicitly prohibits Martingale strategies.
*   **Consistency:** The evaluation process emphasizes validating the consistency of your performance, implying that your EA strategy should demonstrate stable profitability.
*   **Risk Management:** Traders must adhere to strict risk management rules, including maximum daily loss and maximum overall loss limits.

**Key Rules for Funded Accounts (General):**
*   **Three-Phase Selection Process:** Traders must pass a three-phase selection process to get a funded account.
    *   **Phase 1 (Trading Challenge):** Achieve a profit target (10% for standard, 20% for aggressive) within 60 days, while respecting maximum daily loss (5% standard, 10% aggressive) and maximum overall loss (10% standard, 20% aggressive).
    *   **Phase 2 (Verification Phase):** Validate consistency over a new 60-day period with the same profit targets and risk structure.
    *   **Phase 3 (Funded Account):** No profit target, but adherence to maximum loss rules (10% standard, 20% aggressive) is required. A minimum of 10 trading days is required, and a maximum inactivity period of 30 days.
*   **Profit Split:** 80% to 90% commission on profits.

## FXIFY

**General Stance on EAs:**
FXIFY allows EAs on their One-Phase, Two-Phase, and Three-Phase challenges. However, they have specific prohibitions and restrictions, especially for their Instant Funding accounts.

**Allowed EA Types/Conditions:**
*   FXIFY allows EAs but prohibits any EA that engages in latency arbitrage, group hedging, or high-frequency trading.
*   Some sources indicate that FXIFY allows the use of Expert Advisors (EAs), grid trading, and Martingale strategies, offering traders a high degree of flexibility. This contradicts other sources that state HFT and latency arbitrage are not allowed. It's crucial to verify the most up-to-date and specific rules directly with FXIFY.

**Prohibited EA Types/Strategies:**
*   **Instant Funding Accounts:** EAs, bots, and copy trading are strictly forbidden in both the Lightning Plan and Instant Funding accounts, as all trading must be manually managed by the trader.
*   **Latency Arbitrage**.
*   **Group Hedging**.
*   **High-Frequency Trading (HFT)**.
*   **Reverse Hedging**.
*   **Coordinated Trading**.

**Key Rules for Funded Accounts (General):**
*   **Max Drawdown Limits:** For Starter accounts, max drawdown is 4% of starting balance; for Expert accounts, 4%-5%.
*   **Consistency Rule:** 30% for Starter accounts and 40% for Expert accounts, meaning trading strategy should not have dramatic fluctuations.
*   **Daily Loss Limit:** 2% for Starter accounts, 3% for Expert accounts.
*   **Profit Split:** Up to 90% to the trader.
*   **Withdrawals:** Can be requested on-demand, typically processed within 24-48 hours.

## Funded Trading Plus

**General Stance on EAs:**
Funded Trading Plus explicitly supports the use of EAs, Algos, and Bots on all of its accounts, both during the evaluation process and after passing the challenge.

**Allowed EA Types/Conditions:**
*   They offer flexibility for traders to employ their preferred methods to achieve success, including EAs, algorithms, and bots, within the program's rules.
*   They do not have restrictions on off-the-shelf EAs, grid trading, or high-frequency trading.

**Prohibited EA Types/Strategies:**
While they are generally flexible, it's always wise to confirm if there are any specific strategies that are prohibited. Some general prohibitions across prop firms include:
*   Copy trading from other persons' signals.
*   Tick scalping.
*   Reverse arbitrage trading.
*   Hedge arbitrage trading.
*   Use of emulators.

**Key Rules for Funded Accounts (General):**
*   **Profit Targets:** Vary by challenge type (e.g., 10% for One-Phase, 8% in Phase One and 5% in Phase Two for Two-Phase).
*   **Drawdown Limits:** Vary by challenge type (e.g., daily drawdown up to 5%, overall drawdown up to 10% for some programs).
*   **Profit Split:** Starts at 80/20, can increase to 90/10 at 20% profit, and 100/0 at 30% profit.
*   **Withdrawals:** Weekly withdrawals are possible, with the first withdrawal possible after 0 or 7 days depending on the program.
*   **Scaling Plan:** Traders can scale their account size by every 10% of simulated profit, up to a maximum allocation of $2,500,000.

**Important Note:** The rules and policies of prop firms can change. It is always recommended to visit the official websites of FTMO, Fidelcrest, FXIFY, and Funded Trading Plus and review their most current terms and conditions, especially regarding EA usage and geographical restrictions for traders in Malawi.
